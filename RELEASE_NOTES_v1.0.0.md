## Vndex 1.0.0 — first release 🎉

A personal visual novel tracker for Android, powered by VNDB. Your lists, ratings and reviews stay on your device; no account needed.

### Highlights
- **Library:** Playing, To Play, Finished and Dropped lists, plus an All tab with list badges
- **Ratings & reviews:** 5-star ratings with half stars, and reviews with a spoiler toggle
- **Browse VNDB:** Popular and Latest presets, a full filter sheet (language, platform, length, year, rating, include/exclude tags) and saved searches
- **Find similar:** tap a tag or use Find similar on any VN page
- **VN pages:** description, screenshots and characters, with spoilers removed
- **Display options:** compact, comfortable and cover-only grids or a list, with filters and sort direction
- **Themes:** Vndex, Dusk and Catppuccin in light, dark or pure black
- **18+ options:** blur sensitive images, hide sexual content tags

### Install
Download **Vndex-v1.0.0.apk** below and open it on your phone (Android 5.0+). You may need to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

### Notes
- Data comes from VNDB.org. Vndex is unofficial and not affiliated with VNDB.
- Lists are stored only on the device. Backup/export is planned for a future version.
